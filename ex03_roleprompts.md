

Objective: Control tone and style with explicit instructions.

Choose one task (e.g., "Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview").

Write two prompts:

    One for a friendly tone

    One for a professional tone

Compare the outputs and note how the tone changes while the content stays consistent.

write two emails for a resignation letter. one with a friendly tone and one with a proffesional tone

1️⃣ Friendly Tone

Subject: Moving On – Thank You!

Hi [Manager's Name],

I hope you’re doing well! I wanted to let you know that I’ve decided to resign from my position at [Company Name], with my last day being [Last Working Day].

I’ve truly enjoyed working here and getting to know such a fantastic team. I’m grateful for all the support, guidance, and fun memories I’ve had during my time here.

I’ll do my best to make the transition smooth and hand over my responsibilities properly. Please let me know how I can help during this period.

Thank you again for everything — I’ll miss working with you all, and I hope we can stay in touch!

Warm regards, [Your Name]

2️⃣ Professional Tone

Subject: Resignation Notice

Dear [Manager's Name],

I am writing to formally resign from my position at [Company Name], effective [Last Working Day].

I sincerely appreciate the opportunities I’ve had to contribute to the team and develop professionally during my time here. I am committed to ensuring a smooth transition and will do everything I can to hand over my responsibilities efficiently.

Thank you for your guidance and support. I value the experiences I’ve gained and hope to maintain a professional relationship moving forward.

Kind regards, [Your Name]
